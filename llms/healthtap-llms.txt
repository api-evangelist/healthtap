# HealthTap

> HealthTap is a United States virtual primary care medical group and telehealth technology
> company. Board-certified, U.S.-licensed physicians cover all 50 states and Washington, D.C.,
> delivering video and phone visits for primary care, urgent care, chronic condition management
> and preventive care, plus 90-day follow-up messaging with the treating doctor, prescription
> and lab ordering, and Dr. A.I. symptom triage. Sold direct to consumers on a monthly
> membership plus a per-visit fee, in network with commercial and Medicare plans, and deployed
> white-labeled or co-branded with employers, health plans, health systems and retailers.

## API status

HealthTap publishes **no public API** as of 2026-08-04. There is no OpenAPI or Swagger
document, no GraphQL endpoint, no AsyncAPI or webhook catalog, no MCP server, and no A2A agent
card on any HealthTap host. Its 2016 developer platform, **HealthTap Cloud**
(developers.healthtap.com), is retired — the hostname no longer resolves in DNS, and
docs.healthtap.com still 301-redirects every path to that dead host. Partner data exchange runs
through contracted integrations (Commure for EHR and care coordination, Health Gorilla for
FHIR-based clinical records), under those partners' APIs rather than HealthTap's.

The healthtap.com edge (Cloudflare) returns HTTP 403 to every non-browser client, so automated
agents cannot read the public site either.
